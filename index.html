05Our Process

7 weeks to a launch-ready brand.

7 週，讓品牌準備好出發。

A clear way of working — from understanding your brand and setting direction to design and application — with a defined goal at every stage.

一套清楚的合作流程，從理解品牌、確立方向到設計與應用，讓品牌建立的每個階段都有明確的工作目標。

7
Weeks週
Week01/ 07
  1. 01

    Discover

    理解品牌

    Brand interviews and market research to understand where your company is today, what it needs, and where it's heading.透過品牌訪談與市場研究，理解企業現況、需求與發展方向。

  2. 02

    Define

    確立方向

    Shape the brand positioning and visual direction — the foundation for all the design to come.整理品牌定位及視覺方向，建立後續設計的基礎。

  3. 03

    Design

    發展識別

    Design the logo, typography, color and core visual system.設計品牌標誌、字體、色彩及基本視覺系統。

  4. 04

    Launch

    完成應用

    Extend the brand into applications, organize the guidelines and final files, and get ready to communicate.延伸品牌應用，整理規範與設計成果，協助企業展開品牌溝通。

* The 7 weeks begin on the official start date agreed by both parties.7 週自雙方約定的正式開工日起算。
